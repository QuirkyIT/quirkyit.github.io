Business AI & automation

Business AI and automation options

AI advice and a small trial

We review time-consuming tasks and check whether existing software can help. You receive a recommendation, with a small trial where useful before committing to a custom build.

Business knowledge assistant

We build an assistant that searches your approved policies, procedures and guides. We set up access permissions and test its answers, with source references so staff can check the original information.

Help with routine admin

We configure tools to help staff prepare drafts, gather information and answer routine questions. We test them on agreed examples and show your team how to review the results.

What your AI project includes

A recommendation you can act on, or an agreed assistant or workflow with testing, training and clear running costs.

Start with a focused review before committing to a custom build. Data access, human review and ongoing costs are agreed upfront.

What happens to our business data?

You agree which information may be used and which services may receive it. Sensitive information can be excluded.

How do we know it helps?

Compare the time and effort involved before and after a small trial, including the time spent checking answers.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Sometimes it will. We test against real examples from your business and build human review into the tasks where an error is expensive. Tasks that cannot be checked safely are left alone.

Will this replace our staff?

That is not the aim. The target is repetitive work occupying people who could be doing something more valuable. Work needing judgement, relationships or accountability is deliberately left alone.
