Business IT options

Cloud file migration

We move suitable files from your server to shared cloud storage, set up staff access and test recovery. We also train your team to use the new setup.

Firewall and network setup

We configure your firewall to separate guest Wi-Fi, staff devices and important business systems. We test the agreed connections and leave your IT provider with the settings and recovery instructions.

Software advice

We compare your current software with suitable alternatives, including open source tools. You receive a recommendation that considers the cost of switching, training and ongoing support.

Website hosting migration

We move straightforward business websites to hosting that needs less server maintenance. We check pages and enquiry links, then hand over ownership and recovery instructions.

What your IT project includes

The agreed setup, documentation and training, with a clear explanation of ongoing costs.

Each project is quoted to suit your systems. Hardware, licences, hosting and ongoing support are listed separately where needed.
