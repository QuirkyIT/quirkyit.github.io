Ongoing IT & security care

QuirkyIT carries out scheduled checks and maintenance on agreed IT and security systems. We test that important functions still work and send you a short report on anything that needs attention.

Care covers agreed systems during weekday business hours. It is scheduled maintenance, rather than continuous human monitoring or an unlimited helpdesk.

Ongoing care options

Network, backup and alert checks

We maintain agreed devices, back up their settings and test that security alerts reach the right people. Your care plan states which systems and checks are included.

Repeat security assessments

We review changes in your public information or repeat agreed vulnerability scans. You receive updated findings and priorities at an interval suited to your business.

Cloud and AI maintenance

We review access and backup checks for agreed cloud services. For AI assistants, we update approved information and test that answers remain useful.

Website checks and content updates

We check that your website and enquiry links work, and make the content updates included in your plan. Periodic checks with separately booked edits are available for sites that rarely change.

What ongoing care includes

Agreed checks, included maintenance and a brief report with any recommended further work.

Care is optional. Your quote lists covered systems, check frequency, included work and extra costs.

Can hardware replacement be included?

A prepared spare and a separately quoted replacement visit are available for suitable equipment. Timing is agreed when booking.

Can our provider take over?

Yes. Documentation and training let your team or IT provider look after it.

Is ongoing care the same as 24/7 monitoring?

No. Care is scheduled work during business hours. It means the agreed checks and tests actually happen on a schedule, not that somebody is watching your systems overnight.

Is this a replacement for a managed IT provider?

No. Care covers the specific systems we agreed, with a defined set of checks. It is not an unlimited helpdesk, and it works alongside your existing provider.
