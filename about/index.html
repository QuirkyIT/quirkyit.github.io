About QuirkyIT

Built on 20 years of cyber security and IT experience.

QuirkyIT draws on 20 years of experience across helpdesk support, networking, systems administration, DevOps, incident response and threat hunting. This breadth brings a practical understanding of how technology is built, operated and secured, and how businesses depend on it every day.

We combine deep technical knowledge with management skills to connect the detail of a security issue to the decisions your business needs to make. That means clear explanations, realistic priorities and recommendations that account for the people, systems and budget available to act on them.

We work with your IT or cyber team to explain the technical evidence, and with leadership to clarify the business implications and options. The result is a shared understanding of what matters, who needs to act and where to focus your security investment.

Advice that works for your business.

Advice connected to your business

We start with what you need to protect: your operations, people, information and business relationships. The work connects technical evidence to the decisions your leadership and cyber team need to make.

Alongside your existing team

Your IT provider or security team can be involved in scoping, the findings walkthrough and implementation planning. Reports and handovers explain the evidence, priorities, responsibilities and remaining gaps.

A clear scope and cost

Each quote states coverage, deliverables, dependencies and fees. Any storage, hosting, licensing or ongoing support costs are identified separately. Additional work is agreed before it starts.

Based locally. Focused on your priorities.
