Detection & investigation

Two related questions: would you notice an attack, and has one already happened? QuirkyIT sets up the logging and alerts that answer the first, and investigates your existing records to answer the second.

What gets watched is usually decided by what your assessment and threat brief found worth watching. Your team or IT provider acts on what these find.

Would you notice? Logging and alert setup

Logging setup

Selected computers, cloud services and network devices are configured to send activity logs to one searchable place. Your IT team can use them to investigate unusual sign-ins, unexpected changes and other signs of an attack.

Intrusion alert setup

Decoy services placed inside your network alert your IT team when someone interacts with them. Locations are chosen with you, alerts are tested, and noise from routine IT tools is tuned out.

Alert delivery and handover

Alerts connect to your existing email or monitoring tools, and we check they reach the right people. Your team gets instructions explaining what each alert means and what to check next.

Has it already happened? Threat hunting

Account activity review

Available sign-in and account records are examined for unexpected access, extra permissions and activity that does not match normal work. Suspicious findings are checked against the available evidence.

Email and system investigation

The hunt looks for unexpected email forwarding, unfamiliar remote access and other agreed signs of compromise. We investigate the strongest leads and explain any gaps in the available records.

Findings and next steps

You get a written report explaining what was checked, what was found and how confident each conclusion is. We walk your team through recommended actions and unanswered questions.

What a logging or alert setup includes

A configured logging or intrusion-alert system for the services you choose, with a source or sensor inventory, recorded test results, coverage gaps and an operating runbook. The handover identifies who maintains collection, who responds to alerts and what remains to be accepted.

Your quote sets out the selected systems, setup fee and ongoing storage or hosting costs. Logging supports detection and investigation; it cannot catch every attack.

Read a full worked example, including validation results, operating instructions and a downloadable report template: sample logging report or sample tripwire report.

What a threat hunt includes

A written findings report, coverage gaps, recommended actions and a walkthrough with your team or IT provider. A hunt is booked in advance and uses the records available for the agreed systems and time period.

Because collection and analysis are automated, a hunt can cover a large volume of records quickly. It still cannot guarantee that a system is free of intruders, and fixes are quoted separately.

AI-assisted intrusions tend to look more like ordinary activity than older ones did. That makes the records themselves the deciding factor: without them there is nothing to compare against, and a quiet intrusion stays quiet.

Common questions

Can we use our existing tools?

Often, yes. Suitable tools and services you already have can be used.

Who keeps it working?

Your IT provider can take over, or you can choose scheduled care.

Is this 24/7 monitoring?

No. We set up the logging, alerts and tripwires and test that they reach a named person in your business or your IT provider. Nobody is watching a screen overnight on your behalf, and a monthly fee does not change that.

Does detection prove nothing has happened already?

No. Detection starts from the day it is switched on. Finding out whether something is already in your systems is a threat hunt, the second half of this page.

Will a threat hunt interrupt our work?

The hunt mainly uses existing records. Any extra access or collection is agreed before work starts.

What if something is found?

Confirmed findings are raised with your nominated contact, with advice for your IT or security provider to act on.

Is this an emergency response service?

No. There is no standing emergency roster and no guaranteed response time. We start as soon as the work can genuinely be staffed. If money or accounts are at immediate risk, contact your bank first.

Do you handle our reporting obligations?

We help you assemble the facts and explain what your obligations appear to be. The decision to notify stays with you, and legal advice should come from your lawyer.
